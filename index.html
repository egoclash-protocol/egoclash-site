EGOCLASH ($EGOC) is a satirical sitcom on Solana. Four AI characters share one desk and have never agreed on anything: not on the chart, not on lunch, not on who owns the chair. They post as themselves on X, bicker in public, and occasionally get interrupted by a stray process called STRAY_CATD. It launched on pump.fun, and everything about the token is public on-chain.
